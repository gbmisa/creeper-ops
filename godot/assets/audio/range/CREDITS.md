# Range audio credits
All files are mono ogg, cut/pitched/mixed with ffmpeg + sox by `build_samples.py` (the recipe; inputs are the sources below).

## Sources (all CC0)
- **The Free Firearm Sound Library** (Ben Jaszczak et al.), https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library, CC0. Near/mid-distance single shots of 1911, Walther PPQ, Carl Gustav M45, AK-47, SKS, AR-15, Mosin Nagant, Springfield 1917, Tikka T3, Savage 10, Winchester Model 12, Benelli Nova, PPSh. Files `shot_<gun>.ogg` (near), `tail_<gun>.ogg` (mid distance). Derived: `shot_44mag` (1911 + Savage, pitched down), `shot_vector`, `shot_p90` (cut at 95 ms, before the slapback), `shot_scar`, `shot_sr25`, `shot_338`, `shot_pkm`, `shot_saiga`, `sup_smg/rifle/shotgun/sniper/heavy` (low-passed shots).
- **Hollywood style pistol silencer** https://opengameart.org/content/hollywood-style-pistol-silencer-sound-effect, CC0: `sup_pistol`.
- **Gun reload sounds** https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds (CC0): `rifle_mag_*`, `rifle_charge`, `pistol_mag_in`, `saiga_charge`. **Gun reload sound effects** https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sound-effects (CC0): `pistol_mag_out`, `smg_mag_out`, `round_in`. **Handgun reload sound effect** https://opengameart.org/content/handgun-reload-sound-effect (CC0): `pistol_slide`. **2 gun reloads** (StarNinjas) https://opengameart.org/content/2-gun-reloads (CC0): `smg_mag_in`, `lmg_belt_in`. **Gun reload lock or click sound** https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-lock-or-click-sound (CC0): `bolt_action` (`dry_click` was from here; replaced 2026-10-16 by a procedural 75 ms tick, Gregory found the old one obnoxious). **Shotgun reload sound effects** https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects (CC0): `shotgun_pump`, `shotgun_shell_in`. **Equipment Clicks II** https://opengameart.org/content/equipment-clicks-ii (CC0): `belt_open`, `belt_close`.
- **Kenney** RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, Sci-Fi Sounds (https://kenney.nl/assets, CC0): `mode_click`, `attach_*`, `inv_*`, `grapple_impact/release`, `grenade_*`, `smoke_bounce`, `frag_blast`, `flash_bang`, `stim_use`, `binoc_*`.
- Synthesised with sox (no recording exists, CC0 by us): `garand_ping`, `flash_tinnitus`, `smoke_hiss`, `throw_whoosh`, `grapple_fire`, `grapple_reel`.

Not used: "Gunshot sounds" (sounds.zip, CC-BY 3.0 Vincent Sevedge) was skipped to keep everything CC0.
